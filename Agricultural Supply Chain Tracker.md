Agricultural Supply Chain Tracker Implement a blockchain - based system to track the agricultural supply chain from farm to consumer, ensuring transparency and food safety.

Key requirements:

- Immutable record - keeping for each stage of the supply chain
- User interfaces for farmers, distributors, retailers, and consumers
- Integration with IoT devices for real - time data collection
- Scalability to handle multiple product lines and regions
- Compliance with food safety regulations
- Data visualisation tools for supply chain insights
- Accessibility features for users in rural areas
