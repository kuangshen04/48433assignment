# Case Study: Urban Marathon Event

## Problem Overview

Marathon running is a popular sport worldwide, with events attracting thousands of participants and spectators. In our case study, we focus on an urban marathon event held annually in a major city. The marathon includes a full 42.195-kilometer race, a half-marathon, and a 10-kilometer fun run to cater to different levels of runners. The photo below illustrates the bustling atmosphere at the starting line of a typical urban marathon.

The logistics of organising such an event are complex, involving road closures, hydration stations, timing systems, and coordination with city services. Runners often travel from various locations, requiring a system to manage registrations, race pack coll ection, and updates on race day conditions.

Marathons are not only about the race itself but also about the experience. This includes the expo held before the race, where sponsors and vendors can showcase their products, and the post - race festivities, which often feature live music, food, and a reco very z one for athletes.

## Business Processes

- Plan marathon routes and categories:
  - Distance {Full, Half, 10K}
  - Participant Type {Professional, Amateur, Wheelchair, Fun Run}

- Accept and manage entries.
- Coordinate with city services for road closures and safety.
- Schedule race day events (start times for different categories, expo, post - race festivities).

- Distribute race packs and timing chips to participants.
- Provide real-time race updates and tracking for participants and spectators.
- Manage volunteer assignments and stations (hydration, medical, information).
- Record race results and publish them online.
- Facilitate a feedback mechanism for participants to improve future events.

## Usage Narratives

## Race Director

As the race director, I want to streamline the marathon organi s ation process. It's crucial that participants can register for the race and receive updates directly through a digital platform.

This would minimi s e paperwork and allow for real-time communication. On race day, if there are any last-minute changes due to weather or other unforeseen circumstances, we need to be able to notify all participants promptly.

We also need to manage the flow of the race to avoid congestion, especially at the start and finish lines. It would be beneficial to have a system that can help us stagger the start times based on the estimated pace of runners.

## Participant

As a marathon participant, I want a seamless experience from registration to race day. I'd like to receive my race pack with all necessary information and my timing chip without any hassle. On race day, I want to know my exact start time, the route, and wh ere I can find hydration and medical stations. If I achieve a personal best, I want to be able to share my results easily with friends and family.

If there are any changes to the race schedule or route, I expect to receive timely notifications.

Additionally, having a mobile app to track my progress and provide my estimated finish time would enhance my race experience.

## Volunteer Coordinator

As the volunteer coordinator, I need to ensure that all volunteers are well-informed about their roles and responsibilities. A digital platform that allows me to assign tasks, provide training materials, and communicate updates would be invaluable. On race day, I need to be able to send out quick updates to volunteers, especially if there are changes in their assignments or if additional support is needed at certain stations.

## Spectator

As a spectator, I want to support my friends and family participating in the marathon. I need access to the race route and information on the best viewing spots. A mobile app that allows me to track the runners I'm supporting and provides estimated times f or when they'll pass certain checkpoints would be fantastic.

## Vendor

As a vendor at the marathon expo, I want to maximi s e my exposure to participants and spectators. I need information on booth setup, event schedules, and participant demographics.

A system that allows me to register for the expo, select my booth location, and access participant data would help me tailor my offerings and marketing efforts.
