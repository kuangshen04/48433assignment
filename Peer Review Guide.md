# Software Architecture — Peer Review Guide

## Peer Review Schedule: Weeks 9–12

Peer reviews are conducted during **MS Teams tutorials**.

| Week | Peer Review | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 9 | Peer Review 01 | Application Architecture |
| Week 10 | Peer Review 02 | Data Architecture |
| Week 11 | Peer Review 03 | Cloud & Security Architecture |
| Week 12 | Peer Review 04 | Architecture Evaluation |

**Assessment value:** 20%

There is [**one peer review in each tutorial from Week 9 to Week 12**](./Tutorial%20PR.md).

---

## Before Each Peer Review

Before each weekly peer review:

### 1. Review the topic

Read the **Tutorial PR 1–4** information for the architecture topic assessed that week.

### 2. Prepare your work

Bring the weekly required project material that your group must present and explain.

### 3. Check your group

Use your **self-enrolled Canvas group number** in the review form.

### 4. Update contributions

Complete or update the **Contribution Table** every week before the peer review.

---

## MS Teams Tutorial Process

The peer review occurs during your scheduled **Microsoft Teams tutorial**.

### 1. Present

Your group presents the weekly project architecture topic scheduled for that week.

### 2. Discuss

The paired group asks questions and examines the work presented.

### 3. Review

Each attending student completes the **Microsoft Forms review individually**.

### 4. Improve

Use the feedback provided by your tutor to revise the next version of your architecture.

---

## Eligibility for Weekly Peer Review Marks

> You must attend the tutorial **AND** submit a valid peer review to receive the weekly mark.

### A valid review must

- Use your correct **Student ID**
- Match your **Canvas group ID**
- Identify the **group ID you reviewed**
- Complete all **qualitative and quantitative reviews**

### Weekly outcome

| Situation | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Attended + valid review | Eligible for your group mark |
| Absent OR no review | **0/5** |

Your tutor’s attendance record and your form submission will be **cross-checked**.

---

## How Weekly Marks and Feedback Work

### Group mark

The reviewed group receives the **mean of the valid reviewer rating scores** submitted by its paired group team members.

Mean of valid reviewer scores = Group mark /5

---

### Feedback and processing

Written comments:

- Help groups improve
- Support moderation
- **Do not change the mark**

Your tutor will share the weekly qualitative feedback and comments to help improve your project architecture.

> Constructive, specific and respectful comments are required.
