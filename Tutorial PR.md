# Peer Review Specification

## Week 9 Peer Review 1 – Application Architecture

### Project Context

Clearly describe project context including the project objectives, stakeholders, risks and constraints.

### Requirement Analysis

Clearly describe system qualities requirements of stakeholders and elaborates on their needs through usage scenarios. Provide justification and rational of choices, alternatives that were considered and reasons why those were rejected.

### C4 Application Architecture diagrams

Provide a set of views that describe and progressively develop the applicationarchitecture, including context diagram, container diagram, component diagrams andcode diagrams (only major classes and interfaces are needed).

### Application Architectural Rationale

Clearly explain how your design adopts quality tactics and architectural patterns for achieving the system qualities requirements.

## Week 10 Peer Review 2 – Data Architecture

### Data Management Requirement

Clearly describe data management requirements and elaborates on their needs through usage scenarios. Provide justification and rational of choices, alternatives that were considered and reasons why those were rejected.

### C4 Data Architecture diagrams

Incorporate data architectural decisions into C4 diagrams.

### Data modelling

Conduct conceptual/logical data modelling and physical data modelling.

### Data Architectural Rationale

Clearly explain justify why/how your design meets the data management requirements

## Week 11 Peer Review 3 – Cloud and Security Consideration in Architecture

### Cloud Adoption Requirement Analysis

Clearly describe the needs of cloud adoption through usage scenarios, elaborating them from XaaS choices. Provide justification and rational of choices, alternatives that were considered and reasons why those were rejected.

### Threat Modelling

Develop a dynamic diagram, focusing on data flow. Provide threat model, including entry points, trust boundaries, list of identified threats.

### C4 Architecture diagrams

Incorporate Cloud, Security and Privacy decisions into C4 diagrams.

### Architectural Rationale

Clearly explain how your design meets the cloud adoption, security and privacy requirements.

## Week 12 Peer Review 4 – Architecture Evaluation

Incorporate the feedback collected during the previous peer review for improving the initial architecture design to the final architecture design, and conduct evaluation through the following aspects:

### Architectural context

The architectural context is well described.

### System qualities

The effect of the main required system qualities is well explained.

### Significant scenarios

Scenarios illustrate how the most important competing qualities are resolved by the architecture.

### Architecture alternatives

At least one alternative architectural response to the significant scenarios is described.
